# Contributor Code of Conduct

As contributors and maintainers of the **VMware Virtualization Optimization Toolkit**, we pledge to create a welcoming and respectful community.

---

## 🌍 Our Standards

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and inclusive environment for everyone, regardless of:
- Age, gender, or identity
- Ethnic background or culture
- Level of technical experience
- Professional or academic affiliation

Examples of positive behavior:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Offering constructive feedback
- Focusing on collaboration and knowledge sharing
